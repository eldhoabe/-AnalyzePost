# Privacy

**Should I Read This?** scores text you select and gives you a READ / MAYBE / SKIP verdict. This page explains what happens to that text.

*Last updated: 2026-10-30*

## The short version

- Nothing is sent until **you** choose **Analyze this post** (or press `Alt+A`).
- Then only the text you selected is sent to our server, and on to our AI provider, to produce the verdict.
- **We don't save it.** We don't store the text, the verdict, or a history of what you analyzed on our servers.
- We don't read the pages you browse, and we don't run in the background.

## What is sent, and when

When you analyze text, the extension sends:

- the text you selected (long selections may be cut to a maximum length)

That is all. We don't send the page's URL, your browsing history, your account details or anything else on the page.

## Who processes it

To produce the verdict, your selected text passes through:

1. **Our server**, which receives the request and forwards it.
2. **OpenRouter**, the service our server uses to reach the model.
3. **The Jev decision model** (from TypeSafe AI), which scores the text.

These providers process the text to return a result. Each has its own privacy policy and data practices, which we don't control. Please read theirs if you want to know how they handle data.

## What we keep

- **On our servers:** nothing about your text or your results.
- **Temporarily, to keep the service working:** our server may handle your request's technical details (such as your IP address) in memory to enforce usage limits. We don't keep them afterwards.
- **On your device:** the extension stores your settings. This stays on your device, and you can clear it any time by removing the extension or its data.

## Permissions the extension asks for

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Context menu | To add the **Analyze this post** option when you select text |
| Active tab / the page you are on | To read the text you selected, only when you ask |
| Access to our server | To send your selected text and receive the verdict |

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or share your data.
- We don't use your text for advertising.
- We don't track which sites you visit.
- We don't read text you haven't selected.

## Your choices

- Don't select text and nothing is sent.
- Turn the extension off or remove it any time. Removing it deletes the settings stored in your browser.

## Changes

If this changes (for example, if we add feedback that saves text), we'll update this page, change the date at the top, and ask for your consent before collecting anything new.
